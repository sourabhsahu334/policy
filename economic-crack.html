03Data stored on your device

The App saves a small amount of information in its private storage so it behaves the way you left it:

  • Display preference — light or dark theme.
  • Practice statistics — your test history, scores and daily streak, shown on the Profile tab.
  • An install ID — a random code the App generates the first time you open the leaderboard. It is not your phone’s hardware ID or advertising ID, and it tells us nothing about you or your device.
  • Leaderboard sign-in — if you join, your username, avatar and a sign-in token, plus any finished test results waiting to be sent while you are offline.

Other apps cannot read this storage. It is deleted when you clear the App’s data or uninstall the App.

04The optional leaderboard

The Leaderboard tab lets you compare daily test scores with other learners. It is entirely optional; you can take tests and keep your streak without ever joining.

What is sent when you join

  • Username you choose (3–16 characters). While you type, the App asks our server whether the name is free.
  • Avatar you pick from the App’s built-in set (owl, panda and so on). No photos are uploaded.
  • Install ID described above, so your board entry can be restored if the App loses its sign-in token, for example after a backup restore.
  • Country, always sent as “India”. It is not detected from your device or location.

What is sent after each test, once you have joined

  • The number of questions, correct answers and wrong answers, how long the test took, and the date. Your individual answers are not sent.

Your username, avatar, rank and scores appear on the public leaderboard inside the App, where other players can see them. Choose a username that does not reveal your real identity if you prefer to stay anonymous.

Like any web server, ours receives your IP address with each request; it is used only to deliver the response and protect the service from abuse, and is not linked to your profile.
