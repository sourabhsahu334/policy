JEE Crack Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy covers JEE Crack (package com.neukaps.jeecrack), a practice app for JEE Main previous-year questions published on Google Play by Neukaps ("we", "us"). It explains what the app stores on your phone, what it sends to our server, and how you can remove it. For our other apps and this website, see our general Privacy Policy.

1. The short version

  • No sign-up, email, phone number or real name is needed to use the app.
  • Questions, chapter practice and timed tests work offline, and your progress stays on your phone.
  • We send data to our server only if you join the leaderboard, plus a version check when the app opens.
  • The app has no ads and no analytics, and we never sell your data.

2. Information stored on your device

The app keeps the following in its own storage on your phone. None of it leaves your device unless a section below says so:

  • Practice and test history: scores, questions attempted, your streak and time spent, used for the stats on your Profile screen.
  • Settings: such as light or dark mode.
  • A random device ID: generated by the app, not taken from your phone's hardware. It lets you get back into your leaderboard profile after a reinstall.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all of this.

3. Information sent to our server

Leaderboard (optional). If you choose to join the leaderboard, the app sends:

  • the username and avatar you pick (please don't use your real full name);
  • the random device ID described above;
  • a country code (India);
  • for each test you finish: the number of questions, how many you got right and wrong, and how long you took.

Your username, avatar and points are shown to other players on the leaderboard. If you never join, no scores are sent.

Update check. When the app opens, it tells our server its version number and platform (Android), so we can tell you when an update is available. This request contains nothing that identifies you.

Like any web server, ours briefly records each request's IP address for security and abuse prevention. We don't use it to identify you.

4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app uses Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging to tell you when new questions are added. Firebase assigns your device a messaging token so the notification can reach it; this is handled by Google under the Firebase privacy terms. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's settings.

5. Permissions

  • Internet: for the leaderboard, update checks and notifications. The rest of the app works offline.
  • Notifications: to show new-content alerts. Android asks before granting this, and you can refuse.

The app does not request your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

6. Sharing

We don't sell, rent or trade any information. The only third party involved is Google (Play services and Firebase Cloud Messaging), which delivers updates and notifications. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

7. Data retention and deletion

Leaderboard profiles and scores are kept while the leaderboard runs. To delete your leaderboard profile and scores, email support@neukaps.com with your leaderboard username. We will delete them within 30 days. Everything stored only on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app.

8. Security

All traffic between the app and our server uses HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we keep what we hold to the minimum above.

9. Children

JEE Crack is meant for students preparing for JEE Main, typically aged 15 and over. It needs no personal details to use. If you're under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before joining the leaderboard. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Question content

Questions come from past JEE Main papers, and the worked solutions were written for this app. JEE Crack is not affiliated with or endorsed by the National Testing Agency (NTA).

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its "Last updated" date. If a change affects what the app sends from your phone, we will also say so in the app's release notes.

12. Contact

Questions or requests about this policy:

  • Support: support@neukaps.com
  • General: contact@neukaps.com
